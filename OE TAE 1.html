On-page and technical SEO actions

  • Use one primary keyword in each page title (under 60 characters), meta description (under 160 characters), H1 and the first 100 words.
  • Create a detailed page for every package with day-wise itinerary, inclusions, exclusions, price and FAQs.
  • Add FAQ and Tour schema markup so packages can appear as rich results.
  • Compress images, use descriptive alt text, and keep mobile load time under 3 seconds.
  • Claim and optimise the Google Business Profile with photos, services, hours and review replies.
  • Publish two travel blogs per month, link them to package pages, and earn backlinks from travel bloggers and local directories.

Advertisement design

Three ad concepts cover three goals: a festive sale (conversion), a honeymoon offer (emotion) and a brand video (awareness). All follow the AIDA model: Attention, Interest, Desire, Action.

Tagline

Design tips applied

  • One message and one call to action per ad.
  • Text covers less than 20% of the image, and the headline stays readable on small phone screens.
  • Prices and deadlines are shown clearly to create urgency.
  • A/B test two headlines and two images, then put the budget behind the winner.

Tools: Canva, Adobe Express or Figma for posters; CapCut or InShot for reels.

Email marketing

Email turns website visitors and past customers into repeat bookings at a very low cost per lead. The plan builds a subscriber list, nurtures it with useful content, and sends timed offers.

Building the email list

  • A "Get a free 10-page India travel checklist" pop-up on the website
  • A newsletter sign-up form in the footer and on every blog post
  • Lead forms on Facebook and Instagram ads
  • A booking form that asks for consent to receive offers
  • Tools: Mailchimp, Brevo or Zoho Campaigns

Email segments

New subscribers
Welcome series and popular packages
Interested leads
Itinerary, reviews, limited-time offer
Past customers
Thank-you, review request, refer-a-friend, next-trip deals
Corporate contacts
Group tour and offsite packages

Welcome campaign flow

  1. Immediately1. Welcome

    Introduce the brand, share the free checklist and top 3 packages.

  2. Day 32. Inspiration

    Share a destination guide to build interest.

  3. Day 63. Social proof

    Share customer stories and reviews.

  4. Day 94. Offer

    Give a 10% first-booking discount valid for 7 days.

  5. Day 145. Reminder

    Last-chance reminder and a link to chat on WhatsApp.

Other triggers: enquiry follow-up 24 hours after an enquiry, a post-trip email 3 days after return asking for a Google review and offering a referral discount, and festival emails before Diwali, December and summer.

Sample subject lines

  • Your free India travel checklist is inside
  • Goa from INR 9,999: pack your bags this weekend
  • Riya, your Bali honeymoon quote is ready
  • 48 hours left: 15% off Diwali getaways
  • How was your trip? Tell us and get INR 1,000 off your next one

Best practices and KPIs

  • Personalise the name and send time; use a mobile-friendly single-column layout.
  • One clear button per email, subject under 50 characters.
  • Include an unsubscribe link and honour consent, as required under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
  • A/B test subject lines and send times, and clean inactive subscribers every quarter.
